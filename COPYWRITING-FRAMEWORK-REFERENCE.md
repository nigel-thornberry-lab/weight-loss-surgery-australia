## CORE PRINCIPLES BY MASTER

### **1. GARY HALBERT**

#### **Key Principles:**
- **Conversational tone** - Write like you're talking to a friend
- **Verifiable truth** - Only state what can be verified
- **Specific benefits** - Not "better SEO" but "rank for [your name] keywords"
- **Make it easy** - Remove every friction point
- **Pattern interrupts** - Open with curiosity, not sales

#### **Halbert's Questions:**
- "Who is my target audience?"
- "What do they already believe?"
- "What's the ONE thing I want them to do?"
- "How can I make it ridiculously easy?"

#### **Example from Our Emails:**
```
"I just Googled your name + 'bariatric surgeon.'"
```
- Conversational ✓
- Verifiable (they can check) ✓
- Pattern interrupt ✓

---

### **2. EUGENE SCHWARTZ**

#### **Key Principles:**
- **Awareness stages** - Match message to awareness level
- **Dramatization** - Paint the problem vividly
- **Specific numbers** - "127 searches" vs "many searches"
- **Gap theory** - Show what they have vs. what they could have

#### **Schwartz's 5 Awareness Stages:**
1. **Unaware** - Don't know they have a problem
2. **Problem-Aware** - Know problem, don't know solution
3. **Solution-Aware** - Know solution exists, don't know you
4. **Product-Aware** - Know you, don't know why you're different
5. **Most Aware** - Ready to buy, need final push

**Our Surgeons = Stage 2-3 (Problem/Solution Aware)**

#### **Example from Our Emails:**
```
"Right now, your information is scattered:
→ Some on your website
→ Some on Healthshare (often outdated)
→ Some on AHPRA (hard to navigate)
→ Some on RateMDs (mixed with old reviews)"
```
- Dramatizes the problem ✓
- Specific examples ✓
- Shows the gap ✓

### **3. DAVID OGILVY**

#### **Key Principles:**
- **Clarity over cleverness** - Simple, direct language
- **Research-driven** - Base claims on facts
- **Factual credibility** - No hype, just truth
- **Specific benefits** - Concrete, not vague
- **Long copy works** - If it's interesting

#### **Ogilvy's Rules:**
- Never use jargon
- Never make claims you can't prove
- Never assume they know what you're talking about
- Always give them a reason to believe

#### **Example from Our Emails:**
```
"I built you a verified profile that consolidates everything:
✓ AHPRA-verified credentials (current)
✓ All hospital affiliations (confirmed)
✓ Professional memberships (verified)
✓ Years of experience (accurate)
✓ Specializations (clear)"
```
- Specific benefits ✓
- Factual ✓
- Clear ✓
- No hype ✓

### **4. MAXWELL SACKHEIM**

#### **Key Principles:**
- **"Reason why"** - Always explain your motivation
- **Logical progression** - Lead them step by step
- **Question-based hooks** - Start with curiosity
- **Transparency** - Show your cards

#### **Sackheim's Formula:**
1. Get attention (question/curiosity)
2. Show the problem
3. Present the solution
4. Explain why you're doing this
5. Make the offer
6. Remove risk
7. Call to action

#### **Example from Our Emails:**
```
"Here's why I did this...

I'm building Australia's most comprehensive bariatric surgery
resource. Patients need accurate information. Surgeons need
qualified inquiries. This solves both."
```
- Explains motivation ✓
- Transparent ✓
- Logical ✓

### **5. JOANNA WIEBE**

#### **Key Principles:**
- **Voice of customer** - Use their words, not yours
- **Authenticity over manipulation** - Be real
- **Objection handling** - Address concerns head-on
- **Breakthrough-or-bust boldness** - Take a stand

#### **Wiebe's Techniques:**
- Mine customer reviews for language
- Address objections before they're asked
- Show vulnerability (builds trust)
- No fake urgency or scarcity

#### **Example from Our Emails:**
```
"I'm genuinely curious why.

Is it:
→ Not relevant to your practice?
→ Timing is bad right now?
→ You don't trust this is legitimate?
→ Something else I'm missing?"
```
- Authentic curiosity ✓
- Addresses objections ✓
- Vulnerable ✓
- No manipulation ✓

### **6. CHASE DIMOND**

#### **Key Principles:**
- **Ultra-specific benefits** - Not "SEO boost" but "rank for [your name] approach keywords"
- **Objection handling** - "What's the catch?" addressed immediately
- **Value-first approach** - Give before asking
- **Social proof** - Real examples, real numbers

#### **Dimond's Formula:**
- Specific benefit (what they get)
- How it works (mechanism)
- Proof (example/testimonial)
- Easy action (low friction)

#### **Example from Our Emails:**
```
"Your interview shows your personality and approach. That's how
patients choose you over someone else.

Plus, you can add it to your About page. Helps patients understand
your philosophy before they even call.

Dr. [Early Adopter] did this and his profile views doubled."
```
- Specific benefit ✓
- How it works ✓
- Proof ✓
- Easy action ✓

### **7. CHRIS ORZECHOWSKI**

#### **Key Principles:**
- **Conversational storytelling** - Tell, don't sell
- **Soft closes** - No hard pressure
- **"Sell the click"** - Just get them to the next step
- **Personality** - Let your voice shine

#### **Orzechowski's Style:**
- Short sentences
- Conversational tone
- Stories over features
- Multiple CTAs (pick one)

#### **Example from Our Emails:**
```
"Pick whichever feels most natural for your website.

Or don't share it at all - that's fine too. The interview is
live on your profile and working regardless.

Let me know if you want help writing a blog post about it."
```
- Soft close ✓
- Multiple options ✓
- No pressure ✓
- Conversational ✓

---

### **8. LUCIANO VITERALE**

#### **Key Principles:**
- **Ultra-brevity** - Cut everything unnecessary
- **Low-friction CTAs** - One-word replies
- **Easy opt-outs** - Make saying no easy
- **Respect their time** - Get to the point

#### **Viterale's Techniques:**
- One-word reply options ("YES", "NO", "LATER")
- Ultra-short subject lines
- Bullet points over paragraphs
- Clear, simple asks

#### **Example from Our Emails:**
```
"If you want to keep it, just reply 'KEEP' and I'll hold it.

If you're not interested, reply 'REMOVE' and I'll delete it.

If you're interested but timing is bad, reply '3 MONTHS'
and I'll follow up then."
```
- One-word replies ✓
- Ultra-low friction ✓
- Easy opt-outs ✓
- Respects time ✓

## ALEX HORMOZI'S OFFER FRAMEWORK

### **The Value Equation:**

```
VALUE = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement)
        ÷ (Time Delay + Effort & Sacrifice)
```

### **How to Increase Value:**

**1. Increase Dream Outcome**
- Make the result more desirable
- "Verified profile + interview = differentiation"

**2. Increase Perceived Likelihood**
- Social proof (33 surgeons verified)
- Examples (Dr. [Name] saw results)
- Guarantee (we handle everything)

**3. Decrease Time Delay**
- "Takes 5 minutes to review"
- "Profile goes live immediately"
- "Interview published in 48 hours"

**4. Decrease Effort & Sacrifice**
- "We built it for you"
- "We handle updates"
- "Zero cost"

### **Offer Stacking:**

Don't just offer one thing. Stack multiple offers:

**Tier 1:** Verified profile
**Tier 2:** + Interview
**Tier 3:** + Blog feature
**Tier 4:** + Co-authored content
**Tier 5:** + Expert quotes

Each tier adds value without adding cost.

### **Example from Our Emails:**

```
"Your profile includes:
✓ AHPRA verification badge
✓ Full credentials display
✓ Hospital affiliations
✓ Patient inquiry routing
✓ SEO optimization
✓ Quarterly updates

Plus, I'll invite you to do an interview (optional).

Plus, I'll turn it into a blog post.

Plus, you can share it on your website.

Worth: $10,000+/year
Cost: $0
Time: 5 minutes"
```

## EMAIL STRUCTURE TEMPLATE

### **Subject Line:**
- Curiosity-driven (Halbert)
- Specific (Schwartz)
- Personal (Wiebe)

**Examples:**
- "Dr. [Name] - I built something for you"
- "I Googled you and found something missing"
- "Quick question about your [Hospital] practice"

---

### **Opening (First 3 Lines):**
- Pattern interrupt (Halbert)
- Verifiable truth (Ogilvy)
- Conversational (Orzechowski)

**Examples:**
- "I just Googled your name + 'bariatric surgeon.'"
- "Quick question: Have you seen your Healthshare listing lately?"
- "I noticed something missing when I searched for you online."

---

### **Problem Definition (Middle):**
- Dramatize the problem (Schwartz)
- Specific examples (Dimond)
- Show the gap (Halbert)

**Examples:**
- "Your information is scattered across 4+ sites"
- "Patients can't easily verify your credentials"
- "They're making a $15,000 decision based on incomplete info"

---

### **Solution Presentation:**
- Clear benefits (Ogilvy)
- Specific outcomes (Dimond)
- Easy to understand (Wiebe)

**Examples:**
- "I built you a verified profile that consolidates everything"
- "One place. Verified. Easy to compare."
- "Takes 5 minutes to review"

---

### **Reason Why:**
- Explain motivation (Sackheim)
- Transparent business model (Wiebe)
- Builds trust (Ogilvy)

**Examples:**
- "I'm building Australia's most comprehensive directory"
- "We make money from advertising, not from surgeons"
- "Your verified profile makes our directory more valuable"

---

### **Call to Action:**
- Ultra-low friction (Viterale)
- Multiple options (Orzechowski)
- Easy opt-outs (Wiebe)

**Examples:**
- "Reply 'YES' to proceed"
- "Click here to review: [LINK]"
- "Or reply 'REMOVE' if not interested"

---

### **P.S. Section:**
- Social proof (Dimond)
- Additional benefit (Hormozi)
- Soft urgency (Schwartz)

**Examples:**
- "P.S. Dr. [Competitor] verified yesterday"
- "P.S. After you approve, I'll invite you to do an interview"
- "P.S. If timing is bad, reply 'LATER' and I'll follow up"

## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

### **1. Fake Numbers**
❌ "127 searches last month" (can't verify)
✓ "I Googled your name" (verifiable)

### **2. Vague Benefits**
❌ "Better SEO"
✓ "Rank for '[your name] approach' keywords"

### **3. Hype Language**
❌ "Amazing opportunity!"
✓ "Worth considering"

### **4. Hidden Motivation**
❌ Not explaining why you're doing this
✓ "I need backlinks to build directory authority"

### **5. High Friction**
❌ "Fill out this form with 20 fields"
✓ "Reply 'YES'"

### **6. No Opt-Outs**
❌ "This is your last chance!"
✓ "Reply 'REMOVE' if not interested"

### **7. Manipulation**
❌ "Only 3 spots left!" (fake scarcity)
✓ "33 surgeons have verified" (real social proof)

### **8. Jargon**
❌ "Increase your domain authority"
✓ "Help your website rank higher in Google"

## TESTING & OPTIMIZATION

### **What to Test:**

**Subject Lines:**
- Curiosity vs. Benefit vs. Personal
- Short vs. Long
- Question vs. Statement

**Opening Lines:**
- Pattern interrupt vs. Direct benefit
- Question vs. Statement
- Personal vs. General

**Call to Action:**
- Single vs. Multiple options
- Button vs. Link vs. Reply
- Specific vs. General

**Length:**
- Short (200 words) vs. Long (500+ words)
- Bullets vs. Paragraphs
- One ask vs. Multiple asks

### **Metrics to Track:**

1. **Open Rate** (subject line effectiveness)
2. **Click Rate** (body copy effectiveness)
3. **Reply Rate** (CTA effectiveness)
4. **Conversion Rate** (overall effectiveness)

### **Optimization Process:**

1. **Baseline** - Send to 10 surgeons
2. **Analyze** - What worked? What didn't?
3. **Hypothesis** - "If I change X, Y will improve"
4. **Test** - Send variation to next 10 surgeons
5. **Measure** - Compare results
6. **Iterate** - Keep winner, test new variation
