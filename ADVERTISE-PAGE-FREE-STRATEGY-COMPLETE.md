# Advertise Page Transformed to Free Strategy ✅

## Summary

The `/advertise` page has been completely transformed from a **paid subscription model** ($89/month) to a **100% free value stack model** focused on beating the cold start problem through backlinks and interview content.

---

## Strategic Shift

### **Before: Paid Subscription Focus**
- Headline: "Get Found By Patients... Featured surgeons get premium placement for $89/month"
- Three pricing tiers: Monthly ($89), Annual ($720), Early Bird ($89 locked)
- Value justification comparing to Google Ads and SEO agencies
- Limited time offer for badge placement

### **After: Free Value Stack Focus**
- Headline: "Free Verified Profile + Interview Feature + SEO Backlinks"
- Zero pricing - everything is 100% free
- Two-tier value stack: Verified Profile ($13,600 value) + Interview Feature ($12,000 value)
- Badge placement positioned as SEO benefit (reciprocal backlinks)
- Total value: $25,600+/year for 30 minutes of surgeon time
