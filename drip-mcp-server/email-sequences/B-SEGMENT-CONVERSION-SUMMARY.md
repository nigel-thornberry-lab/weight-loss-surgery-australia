## CTA STRATEGY CHANGES

### **OLD STRATEGY:**
- Primary: "View verified surgeons in {{location}}"
- Secondary: "Get help booking" (passive, optional)
- Tertiary: Various browse/research CTAs
- **Result:** Low friction but also low conversion

### **NEW STRATEGY:**
- Primary (only): "Book a matching call"
- Emphasis: "We call YOU" (lower friction than "call us")
- Focus: Matching service + coordination + time-saving
- Positioning: We're the matchmaker, not just a directory
- **Result:** Higher friction entry but much stronger conversion intent

---

## PSYCHOLOGICAL PRINCIPLES APPLIED

### **Decision Paralysis Breaking:**
- "You're overthinking this"
- "There's no perfect surgeon"
- "Stop researching - we've done the work"

### **Time Scarcity (Real, Not Fake):**
- "You've spent 2 days/10 days/2 weeks researching"
- "Could be meeting surgeon next week"
- "Another year goes by..."

### **Effort Comparison:**
- "DIY: 15-20 hours" vs "Us: 15 minutes"
- "DIY: $600-900 consultations" vs "Us: $0 upfront"
- Makes the choice obvious

### **Social Proof:**
- "47 patients matched this month"
- "Average time to booked consultation: 16 hours"
- "Patients save 10-15 hours of research"

### **Fear Addressing (Email 7B):**
- "Maybe the real reason is fear..."
- "That's normal. Everyone feels this."
- "Fear doesn't go away by waiting"
- "Next small step is just a consultation"
